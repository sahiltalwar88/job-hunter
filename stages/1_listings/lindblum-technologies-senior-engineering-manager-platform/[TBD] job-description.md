# Senior Engineering Manager, Platform — Lindblum Technologies

## Company Overview
Lindblum Technologies is a Series C platform engineering company building the internal developer platform that powers some of the fastest-growing tech companies. Our platform unifies CI/CD, service ownership, and developer productivity tooling into a single self-service experience. We serve over 500 engineering teams and process millions of deployments per month. Our mission is to make every engineering team more productive by eliminating toil and friction. Named after the industrial capital of our founder's home region, we bring engineering rigor to developer experience.

## Role Description
We are looking for a Senior Engineering Manager to lead our Platform Experience team. You will own the internal developer platform, CI/CD tooling, and service catalog that hundreds of engineering teams rely on daily. This role focuses on developer productivity, self-service automation, and building a culture of engineering excellence across the organization.

## Responsibilities
* Manage and grow a 20-30 engineer team building internal developer platform, CI/CD tooling, and service catalog
* Drive developer productivity initiatives using DORA and SPACE metrics
* Build self-service deployment platforms that reduce lead time and deployment friction
* Launch and maintain internal developer portals unifying documentation, ownership, and on-call schedules
* Introduce engineering excellence programs spanning code review, testing culture, and on-call practices
* Adopt AI-enabled SDLC practices including code generation and automated code review tooling
* Partner with engineering leaders across the company to align platform strategy with team needs
* Reduce platform cost-to-serve while maintaining or improving developer experience

## Requirements
* 8+ years of engineering experience with 4+ years in engineering management
* Proven track record managing 20+ engineer platform or developer experience teams
* Deep expertise in CI/CD, developer platforms, and self-service tooling
* Experience with DORA and SPACE metrics for developer productivity measurement
* Strong understanding of Kubernetes, container orchestration, and infrastructure as code
* Experience driving engineering excellence programs and culture change
* Ability to balance platform reliability with developer velocity

## Nice to Have
* Experience with AI-enabled development workflows (Copilot, automated code review)
* Background in internal developer portals and service catalogs
* Experience reducing platform cost-to-serve at scale
