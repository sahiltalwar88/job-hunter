# VP of Engineering — Al Bhed Machina

## Company Overview
Al Bhed Machina is a late-stage infrastructure company salvaging and rebuilding the technology that powers the modern internet. We process over 10 billion API calls daily and are rapidly scaling to meet demand. Our engineering organization is at 60 engineers today and we need a visionary VP of Engineering to scale it to 150+ over the next two years. Backed by tier-one investors, we are building the foundation for the next decade of growth. Our mission is to build reliable, high-performance infrastructure that scales with our customers' ambitions — no matter how complex the legacy.

## Role Description
We are seeking a VP of Engineering to lead our entire engineering organization through a period of explosive growth. You will own org design, hiring strategy, engineering culture, and governance across all engineering teams. This is an executive role reporting to the CEO, with responsibility for scaling the organization from 60 to 150+ engineers while maintaining high performance, low attrition, and strong engineering culture.

## Responsibilities
* Own engineering org design and team structure across 60-150+ engineers
* Define and execute hiring strategy with a target of 92%+ offer acceptance rate and low attrition
* Establish engineering governance frameworks spanning metrics, architecture review, and incident management
* Drive adoption of DORA and SPACE frameworks for holistic developer productivity measurement
* Build engineering culture through internal tech talks, engineering blogs, and conference participation
* Mentor and develop engineering managers and directors; build a strong leadership bench
* Partner with the CEO and executive team on business strategy, roadmap, and resource allocation
* Align engineering organization with company objectives through 3-year strategic planning

## Requirements
* 12+ years of engineering experience with 7+ years in engineering management
* Proven track record scaling engineering organizations from 40 to 150+ engineers
* Experience building engineering governance frameworks and architecture review boards
* Deep expertise in org design, hiring strategy, and engineering culture
* Experience with DORA, SPACE, and other developer productivity frameworks
* Strong track record of mentoring engineering managers and building leadership pipelines
* Excellent executive communication and stakeholder management skills
* Experience aligning engineering strategy with business objectives at the executive level

## Nice to Have
* Experience speaking at industry conferences (QCon, CloudNativeCon, DevOps Enterprise Summit)
* Background in community building (meetups, mentorship programs, open source)
* Experience scaling organizations through hypergrowth or IPO preparation
