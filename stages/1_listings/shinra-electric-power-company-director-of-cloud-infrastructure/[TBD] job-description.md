# Director of Cloud Infrastructure — Shinra Electric Power Company

## Company Overview
Shinra Electric Power Company is the world's leading energy and infrastructure conglomerate, powering cities and economies across the globe. We operate the largest distributed power grid on the planet, processing billions of telemetry events per day across multi-region deployments. Our mission is to deliver clean, reliable, and cost-efficient power to every corner of the world. We are scaling our cloud infrastructure organization to support next-generation smart grid technology and real-time energy optimization.

## Role Description
We are seeking a Director of Cloud Infrastructure to lead the platform engineering organization that powers our global smart grid. You will own the infrastructure, reliability, and cost optimization teams that support our core telemetry and energy distribution platform. This is a high-impact role reporting to the SVP of Engineering, with responsibility for multi-region architecture, cloud cost management, and compliance across our infrastructure.

## Responsibilities
* Lead and grow a 30-50 person engineering organization across cloud infrastructure, reliability, and platform tooling teams
* Define and execute the multi-year roadmap for cloud platform services supporting billions of daily telemetry events
* Drive cloud cost optimization initiatives across multi-region deployments targeting 25%+ reduction in infrastructure spend
* Own compliance programs including SOC2, GDPR, and data retention architecture for energy sector regulations
* Establish engineering governance frameworks spanning DORA metrics, incident review, and architecture standards
* Build a culture of operational excellence and reliability for tier-1 infrastructure supporting critical energy services
* Partner with business leadership to align platform strategy with company objectives and regulatory requirements

## Requirements
* 10+ years of engineering experience with 5+ years in engineering management
* Proven track record leading 30+ person engineering organizations at internet scale
* Deep expertise in multi-region cloud architecture (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
* Experience driving cloud cost optimization and FinOps programs
* Hands-on experience with SOC2, GDPR, or similar compliance initiatives
* Strong understanding of Kubernetes, container orchestration, and infrastructure as code
* Experience building engineering governance frameworks and architecture review processes

## Nice to Have
* Experience with service mesh technologies (Istio, Linkerd)
* Background in observability and SRE practices for high-throughput telemetry systems
* Experience scaling organizations through hypergrowth phases
